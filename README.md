An evidence-based, interactive behavioral psychology web application exploring **Future Self Continuity** and **Temporal Discounting**. Grounded in Dr. Hal Hershfield's seminal neuroimaging and decision-making research, this tool helps users bridge the psychological divide between their present and 10-year future self, mapping their trajectory to one of 8 distinct archetypes.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

- **Empirical Continuity Scoring**: Assesses future vividness, emotional connectedness, and decision alignment across key temporal horizons.
- **8 Psychological Archetypes**: Categorizes long-term orientation into nuanced behavioral profiles (Visionary Architect, Cosmic Navigator, Resilient Sentinel, Catalyst Weaver, etc.).
- **1080x1920 High-Resolution Story Card**: Generates an aesthetic, client-side Canvas card formatted for Instagram Stories, Threads, and mobile camera roll via the Web Share API.
- **100% Private & Client-Side**: All scoring, archetype matching, and visual generation run locally in the browser runtime. Zero responses, scores, or personal inputs are sent to any server.
- **12 Global Languages**: Fully localized in English, Korean, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish, Portuguese, German, French, Russian, Hindi, Indonesian, and Turkish.
- **Progressive Web App (PWA)**: Offline-capable via Service Worker with instant responsive mobile layout (390x844 touch-optimized).

## 🔬 Scientific Foundations

Temporal discounting research demonstrates that individuals who perceive their future self as an emotional stranger discount future rewards more steeply, leading to higher procrastination, lower savings rates, and poorer health decisions.

- **Hershfield, H. E., et al. (2011)**. *Increasing Saving Behavior Through Age-Progressed Renderings of the Future Self*. Journal of Marketing Research, 48(SPL), S23–S37.
- **Hershfield, H. E. (2011)**. *Future self-continuity: how conceptions of the future self transform intertemporal choice*. Annals of the New York Academy of Sciences, 1235(1), 30–43.
- **Ersner-Hershfield, H., Wimmer, G. E., & Knutson, B. (2009)**. *Saving for the future self: Neural measures of future self-continuity predict temporal discounting*. NeuroImage, 44(2), 569–574.
